About EuroAsia Development

A leading global force in paper trade

Founded in Hong Kong in 2017, EuroAsia Development Limited specializes in the international trade of paper and packaging solutions. Our mission: connect global paper markets through quality products and seamless service.

01

Company Overview

We offer comprehensive logistics solutions and paper trade services, backed by a mature logistics support system built for efficiency and cost-effectiveness. Our success — and the timely, secure delivery of your goods — comes from meticulous logistics processes and an expert team providing tailored support for a variety of client needs.

With deep collaborations with major pulp and paper companies, we supply a full range of products for industrial, cultural and household use. Partnering with us gives you access to real trade expertise and global resources — keeping your business ahead in the market.

02

Global Expertise

Our leadership brings executive experience from APP, International Paper, Graphic Packaging, Magna Group and other industry leaders — with 15+ years in the paper industry per team member.

We maintain a strategic on-the-ground presence with professionals based in Colombia, Guatemala, Turkey, India, Vietnam and the USA. Native-language speakers with deep client networks — merchants, traders, printing houses — engage daily to deliver tailored solutions and rapid response.

03

Vision & Initiatives

Expand product lines, deepen market presence, and drive sustainable solutions across the paper value chain.

Our goal: become the world's preferred partner in paper & packaging trade.

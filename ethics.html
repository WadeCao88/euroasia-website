Ethics & Compliance

Committed to a sustainable future

At EuroAsia Development, we recognize that our actions today have a lasting impact on the environment — so we integrate sustainability into every aspect of our sourcing operations.

Environment

Environmental Stewardship

We minimize our ecological footprint through responsible sourcing practices, partnering with suppliers who share our commitment to sustainability and environmental protection — reducing emissions, promoting renewable materials, and preserving biodiversity.

Fiber over plastic

Paper-Based Solutions

We actively promote paper and fiber-based packaging to reduce plastic usage and its impact on the environment — a core part of how we serve our customers and the planet.

Integrity

Partnerships & Collaboration

Sustainability challenges require collective action. We seek partnerships with responsible suppliers and like-minded organizations — and continuously evaluate our ethical and compliance practices against evolving global standards.

Certification

FSC® Chain-of-Custody certified

Certificate SGSHK-COC-311364, issued by SGS Hong Kong — valid from 30 September 2024 to 29 September 2029, subject to satisfactory surveillance audits.

Scope covers purchasing and sales (Transfer System) of FSC 100%, FSC Mix and FSC Recycled grades across copying, printing and communication paper, wrapping and packaging paper, coated and uncoated paperboard, food wrapping paper, tissues, linerboard/testliner and specialty paper.

Certificate validity can be verified at search.fsc.org.
